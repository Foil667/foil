Official recovery routes

The scanner finds things. Recovery happens in the official apps — never here, never via a DM, never via a link someone sends you.

  • bridge.base.org — official Base bridge: finish or track withdrawals
  • merkl.xyz — official Merkl app: claim unclaimed rewards
  • basescan.org — block explorer: verify any address, tx, or token
  • docs.base.org — official docs, including withdrawal guides
