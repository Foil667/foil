## Finding 1
The SEC has created a new five-year Innovation Exemption that allows trading of tokenized U.S. stocks on public blockchains with shareholder rights equal to traditional shares, according to a Pluang news card published around September 20, 2026 (the page carries a market snapshot timestamped Sep 20, 2026 21:01 WIB; the page itself states no publication date). The piece frames the framework as opening new revenue streams for Coinbase, Robinhood, and Circle beyond trading fees, including custody, issuance technology, blockchain infrastructure, and stablecoin settlement, and specifically notes that Coinbase benefits from its Ethereum Layer 2 platform, Base, while Robinhood must adapt its offshore token model to meet SEC rules. The card describes the new framework as a significant regulatory step that may expand the tokenized stock market in the U.S. For the reader, whose wallet, identity, and NFT holdings all sit on Base, this is the regulatory tailwind behind Base's positioning as infrastructure for tokenized equities.

## Finding 2
Coinbase restored the Coinbase Wallet name on Sept. 10, 2026, ending a little more than a year of branding the self-custodial product as the Base App (Coinbase renamed Coinbase Wallet to Base App in July 2025, per Base's launch announcement). Per The Block, via bitzo's report, the change recasts the app as a multichain trading platform rather than the social-centered 'everything app' introduced in 2025: the revived wallet supports Base, Robinhood Chain, Solana, Bitcoin, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Monad, Optimism, Arbitrum, Polygon and Avalanche, with in-app decentralized-exchange conversions across supported networks. Coinbase Wallet product head Ryan Kass said the product includes onchain perpetual futures powered by Hyperliquid, prediction markets, and tokenized stocks. The report notes the wallet remains self-custodial (users keep their recovery phrase) and that Coinbase withdrew support for Dogecoin, Litecoin, and several legacy networks after Aug. 15, 2026. The article states no publication date; the name change it reports took effect Sept. 10, 2026, so this is a background development for the reader, not today's news. For the reader, Base shifts from being the app's brand to being one network among many that the wallet serves, even as Base remains central to Coinbase's trading infrastructure.
- https://bitzo.com/2026/09/coinbase-wallet-name-restored-multichain-trading-hub (undated)
