## Finding 1
The SEC issued Order 34-106402 on September 17, 2026, granting a five-year "Innovation Exemption" that lets qualifying tokenized securities venues trade tokenized National Market System stocks through permissioned automated market maker pools, effective immediately through September 17, 2031. Robinhood shares closed Friday, September 18, 2026 at $119.82, up 9.12% on 38.91 million shares (the week's busiest session), a 14.75% two-day rally from Wednesday's close; Coinbase shares also climbed roughly 10-11% the same day. Guardrails in the order: Tier 1 venues capped at 75 symbols (250 for Tier 2), monthly volume ceilings of 0.25% of prior-month average daily share volume (2.5% Tier 2), a 30-calendar-day public operating notice before launch, transaction-data updates within 10 minutes, and issuer objection rights that let companies block tokenization of their shares. Tokens must carry the same rights as the underlying shares. The order does not approve Robinhood or guarantee a U.S. launch, and Robinhood has not announced a U.S. venue under the exemption. Robinhood CEO Vlad Tenev said on September 9 that Robinhood Chain reached more than 120 countries, that the chain carries "about 200" stock tokens with "obviously more coming," and that token volumes remain a "vanishingly small percentage" of the equities market; he also said recent chain revenue reached as much as $6 million in one day before partner sharing. Nasdaq trading resumes Monday after the weekend; a Robinhood venue notice, once published, sets its earliest operating date 30 calendar days later.

## Finding 4
SODAX integrated Robinhood Chain into its cross-network execution and liquidity system, whitelisting 17 assets: ETH, bnUSD, SODA, USDG, and 13 of Robinhood's tokenized stock assets (AAPL, GME, MSTR, MU, NVDA, QQQ, SGOV, SLV, SNDK, SPCX, SPY, TSLA, and USO). The 13 tokenized assets are whitelisted not only on Robinhood Chain but also on Sonic, Stellar, and Hedera, and are tradable across SODAX's 22 supported networks as of September 15, 2026, with liquidity coordinated across networks underneath. Any team already building on Robinhood Chain, Sonic, Stellar, or Hedera inherits the full set through one SDK connection with no per-asset integration and no separate liquidity to source per token; trading liquidity is sourced from Robinhood, and SODAX's role is execution and liquidity, not issuance. USDG, the Global Dollar stablecoin, joins SODAX's lending and borrowing markets through the integration; the piece notes USDG has since been adopted as Robinhood Chain's native stablecoin. A HashPack partnership to bring RWAs to Hedera is already in development.
