## Ethereum and Base end joint wallet standard talks
Your wallet on Base may soon run on different transaction rules than the same address on Ethereum. Developers ended the push for a shared account-abstraction standard, confirmed September 15 by Ethlabs researcher Derek Chiang, with [Ethereum advancing EIP-8141](https://www.htx.com/news/ethereum-base-wallet-standards-talks-fail-ethlabs-researcher-lTTG9KVL/) and [Base advancing EIP-8130 already live on its Vibenet devnet](https://www.fxcoinz.com/news/ethereum-base-end-push-shared-next-generation-wallet-standard).
