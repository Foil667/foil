## Most tokenized-stock products need a rebuild for the SEC pilot
The SEC's tokenized-stock pilot opens this week, and the chains leading today lead with products that do not qualify. The September 17 order bans synthetic price trackers, demands permissioned venues, and hands issuers a 30-day veto: [Robinhood's Jersey-issued Stock Tokens](https://memeburn.com/the-secs-new-rules-for-amms-and-tokenized-stocks-which-projects-gain-and-which-have-to-rebuild/), Kraken's xStocks, and Ondo's offshore products sit outside the US lane until they're redesigned. Thursday's trading already sorted the field: Securitize rose 14%, Bullish 10%, and Coinbase 5%, with [Coinbase saying voting rights for its tokenized shares are coming soon](https://particle.news/story/sec-opens-five-year-pilot-for-tokenized-us-stocks-putting-coinbase-robinhood-and-circle-in-focus).
