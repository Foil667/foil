## Ondo lets institutions mint tokenized stocks from shares they hold
Ondo Finance [announced today](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/09/21/ondo-expands-tokenized-stock-access-with-share-based-minting/) that approved institutions can now mint and redeem Ondo Stocks with shares they already hold, through Alpaca's Instant Tokenization Network on Ethereum and BNB Chain. Instead of finding separate cash to mint the tokenized version, an institution's shares move by internal book transfer from its Alpaca account to Ondo's, and the matching tokens are issued onchain. Ondo says the in-kind route should tighten spreads and deepen liquidity across secondary markets, and the trip reverses on redemption: tokens back, shares home.
