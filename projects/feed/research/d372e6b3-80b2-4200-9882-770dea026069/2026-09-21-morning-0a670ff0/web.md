## Robinhood Chain fees fall 97% as transactions hold near highs
Claiming your next free mint on Robinhood Chain now costs about 2.6 cents a transaction. Network fees [fell 97%](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/09/19/robinhood-chain-fees-collapse-97-even-as-transactions-stay-near-record-highs) from the early-September peak while activity fell just 32%, so the chain got cheaper rather than emptier.
