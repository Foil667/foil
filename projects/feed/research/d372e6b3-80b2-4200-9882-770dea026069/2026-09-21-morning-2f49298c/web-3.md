## Finding 1
On September 21, 2026, StoneX analyst Mark Palmer identified four factors behind Robinhood Chain's rapid growth since its July 1 launch, per a research note reported by Catenaa. The chain has grown to nearly $1 billion in total value locked; daily DEX volume reached $1.88 billion on the previous Sunday, and the network accounted for more than half of Uniswap trading volume. Palmer's four factors: Robinhood's brand drawing attention to projects building on the network; its permissionless structure, with token-launch platform Pons facilitating about 10,000 launches a day and roughly 25,000 tokens created September 2; Robinhood subsidizing gas fees for wallet swaps worth more than $5; and a growing link between memecoins and tokenized equities, with launchpad Long.xyz letting community tokens launch alongside Robinhood's tokenized stocks. The piece also reports CoinDesk Research estimates that Robinhood app users account for only 1% to 2% of the network's transactions, with most activity coming through crypto trading terminals, Uniswap, and token-launch platforms. Separately, the stablecoin market capitalization on the chain exceeded $1 billion last week, rising about 12% in seven days and 72% over the previous month; USDG represents about 67% of that total and Ethena's USDe about 30%.

## Finding 2
An analysis circulated by Wu Blockchain on September 20, 2026, puts a hard number on how much of the fees Robinhood Chain collects reaches Ethereum: the chain gathered roughly $4.5 million in fees in a single day while paying its Ethereum settlement layer about $400, as reported by SpendNode. The page itself states no publication date. For every dollar the chain took in, a fraction of a cent went to the base layer that provides its security guarantees, illustrating how rollups capture execution revenue while owing little to the base layer. The piece cautions that this is a one-day snapshot, not a trend, that the $4.5 million is gross fee collection rather than net profit after infrastructure and sequencer costs, and that Ethereum settlement costs swing with blob demand. It also notes ETH traded at $2,577 on September 20, down 1.8% on the day per CoinMarketCap.
- https://www.spendnode.io/blog/robinhood-chain-4-5m-daily-fees-400-ethereum-september-2026/ (undated)

## Finding 3
Anything Labs has issued 100 million UUSD natively on Robinhood Chain, expanding its stock-tokenization and on-chain-finance footprint, per a WEEX report describing developments as of September 2026; the page states no explicit publication date. UUSD (Unity USD) is an AI-native stablecoin launched February 2026, developed and issued by Anything Labs, using USDT as a reserve asset to maintain a 1:1 peg with the US dollar. The company stated on September 16, 2026, that contract deployment was completed on Ethereum, Base, and Robinhood Chain, and gave the Robinhood Chain contract address as 0x61a10E8556BEd032eA176330e7F17D6a12a10000. CEO Patrick said the issuance targets demand for stock tokenization, with plans to list eligible stock tokens on UUSD trading pairs, commit market-making funds with liquidity providers, and expand payment and settlement use. The piece notes UUSD has over $20 million TVL, 5 million cumulative transactions, and 12 on-chain ecosystem integrations. For context: DeFiPrime research from September 1, 2026, found 19 Robinhood stock tokens tracked generated roughly $304 million in 24-hour DEX trading volume, involving targets such as Nvidia (NVDA), Apple (AAPL), Tesla (TSLA), and Hims & Hers (HIMS); RWA.xyz showed the tokenized-stocks market at about $3.01 billion in Distributed Value as of September 20, up 12.43% in 30 days; and on September 17, 2026, the SEC released an 'Innovation Exemption' giving eligible platforms temporary, conditional arrangements for five years to trade tokenized NMS stocks through AMMs and liquidity pools with access controls.

## Finding 4
