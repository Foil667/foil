## Finding 1
Bankr, the agent-economy financial infrastructure that runs on Base and Robinhood Chain, announced on September 17, 2026 that Meta's Muse personal AI agents can now connect to its rails through a publicly available plugin, letting Muse agents create and operate crypto wallets: trading digital assets and tokenized equities, sending funds, launching tokens, accessing Polymarket, and running automated portfolio strategies. The announcement points to Musebook, a live social network built with a Muse agent by developer @wyn_eth, as an early example: it launched its own token through Bankr paired with tokenized META stock on Robinhood Chain, and the token became the currency of 'Musetown,' the platform's internal economy. Bankr founder Sean P. is quoted: 'OpenClaw was for developers. Muse is for everyone. Hundreds of millions of people are about to have an agent, and the only thing standing between that agent and the economy is a wallet. We fixed that. An agent without a wallet can research a trade. An agent with one can make it.' Bankr previously brought the same capabilities to OpenClaw agents; Meta launched Muse itself on September 8, 2026. This release was carried widely across the crypto press (Chainwire original, Unchained, The Bit Times, WalletInvestor).
