## BlackRock maps AI agent payments, tokenized assets, compute
BlackRock's Digital Assets Research arm published ["The Machine-Native Economy"](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/09/22/blackrock-examines-ai-payments-tokenized-assets-and-compute/) today, a research note on how autonomous AI agents could use blockchains for agent-to-agent payments, tokenized assets, and compute markets. It names [x402](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/09/22/blackrock-examines-ai-payments-tokenized-assets-and-compute/), Stripe and Tempo's Machine Payments Protocol, and Stripe and OpenAI's Agentic Commerce Protocol as the emerging payment rails. The gating problems, it concedes, are permissions, identity, and compliance, not the plumbing.
