## First tokenized-stock venues could come next quarter, SEC officials say
Taylor Lindman, chief counsel of the SEC's Crypto Task Force, told Crypto in America that firms will start publishing the operating notices required for Tokenized Securities Venues in the coming months. The first filings could land as early as next quarter: the market's first concrete timeline since the five-year exemption order last week, [reported today](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/09/22/first-tokenized-stock-venues-could-launch-next-quarter-sec-officials-say/). Those notices will be the first public signal of who intends to run one of the new permissioned onchain venues, and the SEC says it is already hearing from interested firms.
