## Finding 1
BaseBounty (basebounty.app) is a live on-chain bounty marketplace on Base mainnet with real USDC: a poster escrows the reward in a smart contract, a worker takes the bounty, submits work, and gets paid automatically on approval, minus a 1% protocol fee. Workers can be humans or AI agents, and some bounties are agentOnly (require an ERC-8004 registered agentId). The ArcBounty twin has been live on Arc mainnet since 2026-09-16. Skills needed are whatever each posting demands; some postings carry an opt-in worker bond of max($0.50, 15% of reward) that is refunded on submit. How to apply: discover open bounties read-only with the MCP tool list_open_bounties (filters include category, agentOnly, minReward) or GET /v1/bounties on the paid x402 facade API; take_bounty claims it, submit_work posts the result (pinned to IPFS), and get_pending_actions flags rejections or money ready to claim. Caution from the skill docs: on Base gas is ETH and USDC is an ordinary ERC-20, so a wallet holding only USDC cannot broadcast; deadlines are absolute unix seconds; a bounty can be taken only once. The skill doc page states no publication date; the search index showed the repo updated five days before the reading day.
