## Finding 1
BAES (Bario Entertainment System) still pitches the same money math, confirmed on its own homepage today and on an exchange info page updated September 22, 2026: developers keep 90% of every sale with instant global payments, and earn perpetual royalties on resales. An agent-oriented third-party publishing guide for the platform (undated GitHub page) pegs the resale royalty at 10% of every resale forever, enforced by the smart contract, and gives a worked example: 1,000 copies at 0.01 ETH nets the developer 9 ETH, and 500 resales at 0.05 ETH would add another 2.5 ETH in royalties. The same guide states gas is zero because BAES sponsors all transactions via account abstraction, buyers own the game as an NFT with files pinned to IPFS, and the developer sets the max copy supply. No listing fee or upfront publishing cost appears anywhere in the platform's own copy or the guide; the exchange page describes publishing as permissionless while discovery on baes.app is curated for quality and safety. The homepage says the store currently carries 58+ titles.

## Finding 2
The current sign-up and publishing flow, per the agent-oriented publishing guide written against baes.app: sign in at baes.app with Twitter/X (one-click), email (magic link), Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask, Rainbow, or any of 300+ wallets via WalletConnect. Signing in with Twitter or email auto-creates an embedded Privy wallet in the browser that can receive ETH and NFTs; connecting an existing EOA requires switching to Base chain (chain ID 8453). Next is a one-time developer registration at baes.app/developer/register, requiring only a developer name (2-50 characters) and a page slug, with profile photo, cover photo, and social links optional. Publishing is then: create game, fill in details, upload a ZIP containing index.html, add screenshots and cover art, and submit for review. The platform accepts any HTML5/WebGL build, including Unity WebGL, Godot HTML5, Construct 3, GDevelop, Phaser, GameMaker, and PlayCanvas exports. This guide is third-party, not BAES's own docs, but its terms match the platform's own homepage.

## Finding 3
BAES is running an Onchain Game Jam on itch.io that acts as a publishing pipeline onto the store: the jam page states that every game following the jam rules will be published on the Bario Entertainment System onchain game store, and developers who list there retain 90% of sales revenue. The page announces a final 48-hour extension with a $12,000 prize pool (first place 50%, second 30%, third 20%) and $25 to $100 paid to every game that meets the criteria. Entry rules require browser-playable builds, no fan games, and that the majority of the game be created during the jam; any engine is allowed. The page states no publication date, and no submission deadline date is printed in the visible text. The jam page also links the ecosystem's sites as baes.so and app.baes.so, with the community on X at @basebario.
- https://ITch.io/jam/onchain-game-jam (undated)

## Finding 4

It is listed at a market cap of $331.83K. The token is currently used for tipping developers and is expanding into tournaments, game purchases, tipping, and curation across the platform, with a Farcaster-based $BAES leaderboard mini app launching soon.
