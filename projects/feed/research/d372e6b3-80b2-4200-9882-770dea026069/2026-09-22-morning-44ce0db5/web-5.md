## Finding 1
Robinhood Chain's daily fee take collapsed 97% from its early-September peak while transaction volume held up: the chain collected roughly $8 million in fees on a peak day in early September, then about $230,000 by mid-September while still processing 8.9 million transactions a day (a 32% activity decline against a 97% revenue loss). The average fee per transaction fell from 64 cents to 2.6 cents. The piece attributes the high-fee period to memecoin launchpads like Pons and trading apps like GMGN, which facilitated 22,600 token launches in 24 hours at peak. Despite the fee collapse, DEX volumes on the chain held or grew: Uniswap V3 volume more than doubled from $2.5 billion to $5.3 billion, and DeFi protocols on the chain processed $13 billion in the week to September 16, up 5% from the prior week. With the Robinhood Wallet gas subsidy ending September 29, the practical read for this week's claims is that on-chain execution is cheap right now — the expensive part of a flip is launchpad and app fees, not network gas.
