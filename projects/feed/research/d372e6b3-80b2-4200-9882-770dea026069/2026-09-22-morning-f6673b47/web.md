## Trueo to move prediction market from Base to Ethereum
Vitalik Buterin publicly welcomed the project to Ethereum L1, praising its decentralization focus and a prediction market that is 'not corposlop.' [Trueo](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/09/22/trueo-to-move-prediction-market-from-base-to-ethereum-as-vitalik-buterin-comments/) says the move is about Ethereum's neutrality, deeper liquidity, and a next-generation oracle for impartial dispute resolution. Existing Base markets keep trading and resolving through the migration, but no new Base markets should expire after January 31, 2027.
