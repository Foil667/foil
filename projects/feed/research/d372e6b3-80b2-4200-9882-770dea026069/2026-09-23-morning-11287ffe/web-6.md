## Finding 1
The Hashgraph Group (THG), the Swiss Web3 and AI firm building enterprise software on Hedera's distributed ledger, announced that its IDTrust self-sovereign identity platform has been validated and officially listed on the IBM Cloud Catalog, putting verifiable AI agent identity directly in front of IBM's global enterprise client base as a purchasable SaaS product. The Cryptonomist's archive dates the story 23 September 2026. IDTrust issues decentralized identifiers (DIDs) and verifiable credentials to AI agents, devices, and humans, built on open W3C standards and designed to support the EU eIDAS 2.0 framework; every credential is cryptographically signed, tamper-evident, and anchored on Hedera's ledger. The platform includes MCP servers so AI agents connected to IBM watsonx Orchestrate can obtain their own identity credentials, keeping each authorization and action auditable. THG also qualified for IBM Silver Partner status and signed a global Embedded Solution Agreement covering IBM cloud and AI technology. THG CEO Stefan Deiss framed it around the 'Know Your Agent' problem: enterprises are deploying AI agents at scale that can negotiate contracts, process sensitive data, and execute financial transactions autonomously, and IDTrust answers the question of how you know an agent is authorized to act on your behalf. The release cites Gartner's forecast that 40% of enterprise applications will embed task-specific AI agents by the end of 2026, up from less than 5% in 2025, and says IDTrust is already deployed with a leading European telecom operator for verified caller identity. IBM has sat on Hedera's governing council since 2019. This is not Helixa or Loopers news - IDTrust is a separate, Hedera-based enterprise identity product - but it is the day's most significant agent-identity development: enterprise validation of verifiable agent identity keeps accelerating in the same category where Helixa's onchain cred stack operates on Base.
