## Bluwhale ships always-on AI agents for tokenized stock trading
Bluwhale [announced today](https://techfinancials.co.za/2026/09/23/bluwhale-delivers-always-on-ai-agents-for-tokenized-stock-trading/) that anyone with a no-code account can build an AI agent that trades tokenized stocks and commodities (gold, silver, oil) around the clock. The limited rollout starts at the end of September through its desktop web app. The agents research market data and act within boundaries the user sets, which puts intelligence and execution in one system in front of consumers: RWA.xyz pegged the tokenized-stock market at $3.06 billion in distributed value on September 20.
