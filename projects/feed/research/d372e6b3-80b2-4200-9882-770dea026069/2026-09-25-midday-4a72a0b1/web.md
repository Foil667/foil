## MoonPay to buy SEC-registered North Capital in $60M all-stock deal
The rails your stock-drip collection will buy from just got a regulated backbone. [MoonPay announced Wednesday](https://cryptonews.net/news/finance/33483663/) it is acquiring North Capital, a Salt Lake City private-markets platform, in an all-stock deal worth over $60 million, subject to regulatory approval. The prize is the license stack: SEC-registered broker-dealer, trading, transfer-agent and investment-advisory operations, which CEO Ivan Soto-Wright says will form the regulatory foundation for mass [tokenized real-world-asset adoption](https://news.bitcoin.com/finance/north-capital-deal-expands-moonpays-tokenized-securities-ambitions/).
