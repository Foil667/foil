## Yuga Labs' Quit rescues 23,155 NFTs, says 660 WETH lost
A bug in Limit Break's Payment Processor V2, the trading protocol Magic Eden ran on EVM from February to October 2024, let an attacker move NFTs at zero price in the small hours. Yuga Labs' 0xQuit spent the night ahead of the drain, relocating 23,155 NFTs to safekeeping (over $5.7M by his count); 660 WETH was gone before he got there. [Magic Eden says](https://cointelegraph.com/news/magic-eden-nft-whitehat-vulnerability) no live listings were touched, but anyone who ever traded on its old EVM market should revoke the stale approval on Ethereum, Polygon, and Base; canceling a listing doesn't clear the operator grant, and the [white-hat account and Revoke.cash](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/09/25/limit-break-nft-exploit-yuga-labs-quit-rescues-23155-nfts-says-660-weth-lost/) agree revoking is the move.
