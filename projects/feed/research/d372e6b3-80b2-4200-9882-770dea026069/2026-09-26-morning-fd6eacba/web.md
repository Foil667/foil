## TALIS splits tokenized stocks into income and upside
TALIS [launched](https://wisevoter.com/world/2026/09/25/talis-launches-structured-markets-robinhood-chain) September 25 on Robinhood Chain: deposit one Robinhood Stock Token and it [splits](https://cryptobriefing.com/talis-onchain-structured-markets-tokenized-stocks/) into an Income token and an Upside token, divided at a cap you choose. Income keeps everything up to the cap plus a premium; Upside takes any rally past it; the upside legs sell in a descending-clock auction whose proceeds flow to Income holders. The underlying token stays locked as collateral, so neither side can be liquidated, and the protocol's only cut is 5% of auction proceeds in USDG.
