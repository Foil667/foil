## GoPlus exposes a second $9M rug factory on Robinhood Chain
A second suspected memecoin rug factory is operating on Robinhood Chain: GoPlus says hundreds of launches routed more than $9 million through a single shared fund-consolidation network over the past 30 days ([cryptonews.net](https://cryptonews.net/news/blockchain/33511236/), [PANews](https://panews.io/articles/01a0e774-0857-72b6-aea4-869d80451734)). The tell is not the classic sudden liquidity pull: tokens launch around hot narratives, spread across brand-new wallets with 4–11 transactions each, and sell in stages through PonsV2Helper and UniversalRouter, with the ETH sweeping through local wallets into a consolidation cluster that funds the next round. GoPlus cautions there is no evidence this crew is the same one behind the earlier $18.43M, 53-launch ring that Wazz traced.
