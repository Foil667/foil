## BlackRock: AI agents could become crypto's next demand engine
BlackRock's Digital Assets Research group published "The Machine-Native Economy" in September 2026, arguing that as AI systems turn agentic they'll pay for data, APIs, and compute natively. [The report](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/09/28/blackrock-says-ai-agents-could-become-cryptos-next-demand-engine/) Bank accounts and card networks were built for humans, not always-on sub-cent machine payments. The paper's own caveat is the interesting half: TRM Labs estimated in September that likely agents account for just 0.6% to 7.5% of volume on one leading agent-payment protocol.
