## Robinhood Chain's August: $1.5B in DEX volume
Free gas on Robinhood Chain ends today, when the wallet subsidies covering transactions since the July 1 mainnet launch expire. [Spartan Capital's August report](https://www.odaily.news/en/newsflash/520256) says the subsidy bought $1.5 billion in DEX volume and $114 million in application-layer fees. Its 27 million funded accounts still have not migrated en masse, so the coming weeks test whether crypto-native volume holds once users pay their own gas.
