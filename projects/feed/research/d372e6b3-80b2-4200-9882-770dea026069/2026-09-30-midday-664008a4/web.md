## Volume halves on Robinhood Chain as free gas ends, TVL stays at $1B
September 28 was the last full snapshot before the subsidy expired: daily DEX volume came in at about $947 million, roughly half the $1.88 billion record from September 13 ([DefiLlama via CryptoCompass](https://cryptocompass.com/articles/robinhood-chain-volume-halves-as-free-gas-ends-tvl-stays-at-1b)). Deposits held firm while turnover faded. With free gas gone as of September 29, every mint claim now pays real ETH gas, which makes the sub-$0.50 gas check the thing to run before anything fires.
