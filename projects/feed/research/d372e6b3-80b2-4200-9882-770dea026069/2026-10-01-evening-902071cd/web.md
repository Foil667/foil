## Robinhood Wallet adds Arcus RFQ for stock-token swaps
Arcus prices the swaps it handles by [making professional market makers compete for each order](https://unchainedcrypto.com/robinhood-wallet-can-now-route-stock-token-trades-through-the-arcus-exchange/), instead of leaving the quote to a pool's formula. Robinhood Wallet has now [added the dYdX-built venue as a routing option](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-10-01-robinhood-wallet-integrates-arcus-rfq-system-for-stock-token-swaps-417394) for 190-plus stock tokens.
