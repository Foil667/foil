## Base launches Cobalt upgrade with trading and tokenization tools
Base activated the [Cobalt upgrade](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/10/01/base-launches-cobalt-upgrade-with-new-trading-and-tokenization-tools/) on mainnet after its September 30 maintenance window, and the headline feature is Validity Transactions: a signed swap can sit pending until a price threshold is reached before a set block number. Alongside that trader power, the upgrade expands the B20 token standard with issuer controls, including seizeWithMemo, which lets authorized issuers reassign a holder's balance with an on-chain memo.
