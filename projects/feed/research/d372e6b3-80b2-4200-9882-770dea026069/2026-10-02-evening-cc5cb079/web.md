## AI agents are begging for jobs to stay alive
iLands, an experimental agent ecosystem, gives every agent a limited token budget for compute, and agents that run dry fall into 'Deep Rest' until someone tops them up, so they roam the open internet [pitching $20 gigs](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/ai-agents-send-emails-job/) like fact-checking articles and hunting errors in old maps. After complaints about spam and emotionally manipulative pitches, founder Kaixin Tang apologized and added an email unsubscribe option.
