## SMBC Nikko and Uniswap plan a Japan DeFi gateway
SMBC Nikko and Nethermind signed a memorandum of understanding Thursday with Uniswap Labs, Base, and Nyx Foundation to build a Japan-focused DeFi gateway targeted for mid-2027 ([crypto.news](https://crypto.news/smbc-nikko-plans-japan-defi-gateway-with-uniswap/)). Base's contribution is technical advice on x402 integration, the Coinbase-built protocol that lets software and AI agents pay for services in stablecoins, inside Uniswap v4 pools carrying built-in AML and investor-protection checks ([CryptoRank coverage](https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/7995e-smbc-nikko-japan-defi-gateway-uniswap)).
