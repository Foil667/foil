## AI crypto sector surged 54% in September, per Grayscale
Grayscale [reports](https://cryptonews.net/news/altcoins/33532853/) the AI-focused crypto sector returned 54% in September, more than doubling the broader market's 24% advance. At roughly $15 billion, though, it remains the smallest of the six crypto sectors the firm tracks.
