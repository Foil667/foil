## Bolsonaro takes Brazil's first round; runoff set for October 25
Flávio Bolsonaro beat Lula 47.2% to 44.9% in Brazil's first round, [Fox News](https://foxnews.com/world/bolsonaro-wins-first-round-brazilian-presidential-election-trump-backed-candidates-continue-red-wave) reports, but neither cleared 50%, so the presidency goes to an October 25 runoff. The runoff decides what happens to the [shelved stablecoin-tax consultation](https://cryptocompass.com/articles/brazil-election-could-change-crypto-rules-after-oct-4) and the central bank's exchange-licensing rules.
