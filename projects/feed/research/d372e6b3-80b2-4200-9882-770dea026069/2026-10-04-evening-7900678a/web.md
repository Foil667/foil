## Hedera CPO lays out trust rules for AI-driven payments
Hedera chief policy officer Nilmini Rubin, writing in OMFIF's October journal, argued that autonomous AI payments need [cryptographic identity tying each agent to the person or institution behind it](https://www.thecoinrepublic.com/2026/10/04/hedera-cpo-outlines-trust-requirements-behind-ai-driven-payments/?amp), so every transaction carries proof of who authorized it. Her operating requirements are fixed, predictable fees and deterministic finality, the rails she says high-volume agent payments need before institutions will touch them.
