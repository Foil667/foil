## Ripple unveils private transfers and AI-agent upgrades for XRPL
AI agents have already driven more than 11 million transactions on the XRP Ledger, and now the chain is getting rails built for them: verifiable identities and spending controls on smart wallets settling in three to five seconds, per [Ripple's Seoul announcement](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/ripple-unveils-privacy-ai-agent-upgrades-for-xrp-ledger-in-seoul). The same roadmap adds zero-knowledge confidential transfers, aimed at institutions that can't run sensitive workflows on a fully transparent chain.
