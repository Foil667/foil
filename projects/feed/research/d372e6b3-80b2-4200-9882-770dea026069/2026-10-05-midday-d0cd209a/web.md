## Arbitrum's record month rode Robinhood Chain's memecoins
Nearly nine-tenths of Arbitrum's record $5.45 million September came from [Orbit licensing fees](https://memeburn.com/arbitrums-record-month-was-robinhoods-memecoin/), the 10% cut Robinhood Chain sends back, racked up during the memecoin rush. Robinhood's gas subsidy for wallet users ended September 29, so October is the first month that shows what demand looks like when traders pay their own gas.
