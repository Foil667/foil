## Mint Chain's last safe withdrawal day is October 10
Mint Chain's withdrawal portal closes on October 20, and anything left behind after that is unrecoverable, according to the [guide that read the operator's fine print](https://cryptocompass.com/articles/mint-chain-closes-its-withdrawal-portal-on-october-20-october-10-is-the-last-safe-day-to-submit). Withdrawals settle in weekly batches and can take up to ten days to reach Ethereum, which is why the guide puts the safe filing date on October 10.
