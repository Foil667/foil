## Robinhood puts $25M of bitcoin on its balance sheet
Johann Kerbrat, Robinhood's senior vice president and general manager of crypto and international, disclosed the purchase on October 7 at the Digital Asset Summit Asia in Singapore ([Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/robinhood-puts-25-million-of-bitcoin-on-its-balance-sheet-executive-says/)). It is the brokerage's first bitcoin held as its own asset, which Kerbrat framed as aligning the company with the crypto community.
