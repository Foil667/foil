## Solana's x402 launches batch USDC payments for AI agents
AI agents on Solana can now open a payment channel, sign a USDC voucher per API call off-chain, and settle everything in a single on-chain transaction, after PayAI Network and [BlockRunAI took x402 batch settlement live on October 7](https://www.odaily.news/en/newsflash/523008). BlockRunAI says routing its AI model endpoint through the scheme saves it over $20,000 a month in network fees.
