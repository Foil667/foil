## Base vault's $6M exploit exposes a disclosure gap
The attacker used the vault's own Safe multisig to add a malicious contract to its lending whitelist, then drained about $6 million via Aave V3. The week before the attack, a researcher had already flagged the whitelist weakness, [Immunefi's head of security Gonçalo Magalhães](https://crypto.news/base-vault-6m-exploit-exposes-disclosure-gap-immunefi/) says, but with the vault's operators unidentified there was no clear channel to report it through.
