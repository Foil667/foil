## Crypto groups sue Illinois over its 0.2% digital asset tax
The Crypto Council for Innovation and the Blockchain Association [sued Illinois officials Friday in Sangamon County](https://cointelegraph.com/news/cryptocurrency-organizations-oppose-illinois-digital-asset-tax-court) over the state's 0.2% digital asset tax, which charges transaction volume rather than income and takes effect January 1, 2027. The state's [draft rules](https://www.mondaq.com/unitedstates/tax-authorities/1852358/on-the-blockchain-gang-what-illinois-draft-digital-asset-tax-rules-would-require) exclude NFTs from the tax entirely.
