## AI agent helped hack nine South Korean banks, CrowdStrike says
[CrowdStrike's report](https://superhits979.com/2026/10/07/suspect-behind-south-korea-bank-hacks-may-be-26-year-old-in-china-cybersecurity-firm-says/) traced the attacks on at least nine South Korean banks to a suspected 26-year-old in Guangdong who paired ARTEX, a Chinese open-source AI penetration-testing tool, with Anthropic's Claude Code. The case is pushing cyber insurers to decide whether an autonomous AI system even counts as a cyber attacker under their policies.
