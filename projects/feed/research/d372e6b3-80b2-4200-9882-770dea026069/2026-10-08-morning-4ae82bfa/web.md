## Buterin warns against rushed wallet moves amid AI threat
In an October 8 post on X, Vitalik Buterin said crypto users [should not scramble to move their funds into new wallets](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/10/08/vitalik-buterin-warns-against-rushed-wallet-moves-as-ai-raises-crypto-risks/) over fears that AI-accelerated mathematics could threaten today's cryptographic systems. He added that keeping funds in addresses that have never signed a transaction is a sensible precaution when it is easy, since the public key stays hidden until the address is used.
