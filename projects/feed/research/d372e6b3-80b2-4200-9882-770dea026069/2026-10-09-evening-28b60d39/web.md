## Fungo Labs opens a free whitelist for 500 encrypted Ethereum NFTs
Fungo Labs has opened the whitelist for [its Genesis collection](https://cryptoworldheadline.com/fungo-labs-genesis-nft-airdrop/): five hundred approved applicants each mint one fully-encrypted NFT, paying only Ethereum gas. Entry is an X-based application with social tasks, three questions, and a manual review, though no mint date or contract address is published yet, so there is nothing to connect a wallet to today.
