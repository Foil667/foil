## Robinhood explores a tokenized actively managed ETF with T. Rowe Price
A $1.9 trillion asset manager is now testing whether a fund it actively runs can live on Robinhood Chain as a Stock Token. Robinhood Crypto [announced](https://crypto.news/robinhood-explores-tokenized-etf/) the exploration with T. Rowe Price Digital Assets, which would make it the first Stock Token tied to a managed portfolio rather than an index, though no ticker, fees, underlying fund, or launch date were named and the company cautioned there is no assurance the needed approvals will come through.
