## Ledger probes $86M in wallet drains linked to reseller
Ledger told its Southeast Asia reseller CryptoBilis to halt all sales and shipments Friday while it investigates [reports of more than $86 million in crypto drained from wallets tied to devices bought through that channel](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/10/09/ledger-investigates-potential-wallet-tampering-after-reports-of-usd86-million-in-crypto-stolen). Recent buyers are advised not to set up their devices, or to move assets to a new device with a fresh recovery phrase if they already did, while the cause stays unconfirmed.
