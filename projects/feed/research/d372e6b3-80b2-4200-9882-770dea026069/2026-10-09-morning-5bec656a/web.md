## XRP Ledger's atomic batching upgrade goes live today
The XRP Ledger's Batch V1.1 amendment, XLS-56, is scheduled to activate on mainnet today, letting developers bundle up to eight transactions from multiple accounts into a single batch that succeeds or fails as one, after clearing the 80% validator-support threshold ([CCN](https://www.ccn.com/education/crypto/xrp-ledger-batch-v1-1-atomic-multi-account-transactions/)). AgentTrust is [upgrading its NFT delivery-vs-payment flow on launch day](https://dev.to/eamwhite1/atomic-nft-delivery-vs-payment-is-coming-to-agenttrust-on-9-october-2026-3jda), wrapping an agent's NFT transfer and XRP payment in one all-or-nothing batch so neither side of the trade can settle without the other.
