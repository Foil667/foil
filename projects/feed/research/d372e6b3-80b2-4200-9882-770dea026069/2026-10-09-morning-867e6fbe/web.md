## U.S. moves $1 billion in bitcoin from Bitfinex hack wallet
The U.S. government moved 12,267 bitcoin, about $1.01 billion, from a wallet holding funds seized in the 2016 Bitfinex hack into new, unlabeled addresses on Thursday, per Arkham data [reported by CoinDesk](https://Www.coindesk.com/business/2026/10/08/u-s-government-moves-usd1-billion-in-bitcoin-from-bitfinex-hack-wallet-no-sale-indicated). No exchange deposit was recorded, so the move reads as reshuffling, which is what a March 2025 executive order prescribes for forfeited bitcoin: a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve that is not to be sold.
