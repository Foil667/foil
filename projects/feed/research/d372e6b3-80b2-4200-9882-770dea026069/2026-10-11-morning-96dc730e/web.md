## EXIT HOUSE launches, letting holders exit dead NFTs for EXIT
A new protocol called EXIT HOUSE [went live on Ethereum mainnet on Oct 9](https://www.tradingview.com/news/chainwire:077d05c20094b:0-exit-house-launches-on-ethereum-letting-holders-exit-unwanted-nfts-and-tokens-for-exit/), letting holders permanently retire unwanted NFTs and ERC-20 tokens for EXIT, its new ERC-20 token. Exits made before Nov 8 earn 1,000 EXIT per $1 of qualifying value, ten times the permanent rate, though only Ethereum NFTs from established OpenSea collections qualify.
