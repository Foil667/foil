## Viral 'SHIB airdrop' posts are a wallet drainer, veteran warns
Shiba Eternity adviser Mazrael warned on October 12 that a wave of fake SHIB airdrop posts flooding social timelines is a wallet drainer, urging users not to click the links or interact with them. The posts allegedly drive users to fraudulent sites that request wallet connections, token approvals, or signatures, though no victims, stolen assets, or malicious domain have yet been identified, per [U.Today's report](https://u.today/fake-shiba-inu-shib-alert-viral-airdrop-exposed-as-wallet-drainer-by-veteran).
