## Based reports card breach to regulators and police
Based co-founder Edison [said on October 12](https://www.panews.io/articles/01a12e2c-5516-73c3-9b9e-94282e2063fb) that the company has reported its crypto card data breach to the PDPC and relevant regulators and has filed a police report. The breach began October 5, when an unauthorized party accessed the internal dashboard for the Based Visa Card program and may have obtained cardholders' KYC data, including names, ID or passport numbers, dates of birth, and addresses.
