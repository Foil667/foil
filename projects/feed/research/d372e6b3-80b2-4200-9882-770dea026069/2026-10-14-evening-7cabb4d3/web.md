## Perpetuals to offer tokenized US stocks under SEC innovation exemption
Perpetuals.com (NASDAQ:PDC) [announced today](https://www.newswire.com/news/perpetuals-moves-to-offer-tokenized-u-s-stocks-under-sec-innovation-exemption) plans to offer tokenized U.S. equities under the SEC's new five-year Innovation Exemption for onchain trading of National Market System stocks. The team built the first regulatory-approved tokenized stocks in 2021, putting 24/7 share trading on Binance, FTX and Bittrex.
