## Brooklyn DA dismantles infrastructure of Russian-targeted crypto scam
The crew behind a Russian-language crypto investment fraud didn't settle for stealing once: weeks after locking victims out of their accounts, they called back posing as recovery agents who could get the money back. The playbook surfaced in [today's takedown](https://www.brooklynda.org/2026/10/14/brooklyn-das-virtual-currency-unit-dismantles-infrastructure-of-multinational-crypto-investment-scam-that-targeted-russian-speakers/), in which Brooklyn's district attorney and the FBI seized 241 domains and 112 servers after a three-year investigation.
