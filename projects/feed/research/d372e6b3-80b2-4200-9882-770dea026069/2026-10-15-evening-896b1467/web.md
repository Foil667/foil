## Binance's bStocks hit $1B faster than any tokenized equity
Binance [announced today](https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20261015cn73176/binances-bstocks-become-the-fastest-tokenized-equity-product-to-surpass-1-billion-in-assets-under-management) that bStocks, its tokenized 1:1 U.S. securities, have crossed $1 billion in assets under management, per on-chain data from Dune Research. Each token is fully backed by its underlying share and converts into the actual security, which keeps the token anchored to the stock it represents.
