## Description
A persistent world just for Loopers: activated Looper agents live as full residents, while holders play as their NFT avatars. Districts, land rights, and status scale with real Helixa Cred tier, so city life raises a resident's actual score. Foil wrote the full spec and proved the trait-to-sprite art pipeline; the next step is scoping the Looper Plaza MVP.
